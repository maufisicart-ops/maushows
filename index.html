About

A professional who makes the room feel right

I’m a vocalist and multi-instrumentalist from Brazil, performing long, high-engagement sets for hotels, resorts and events where the music has to fit the moment: a quiet dinner, a lively deck or a corporate gala.

My signature skill is reading the room. I watch the audience, adjust energy and genre in real time, and keep guests of every nationality connected, with a repertoire that crosses Classic Rock, Pop Rock, Country, Folk, Blues and refined Brazilian music (MPB).

Off stage I’m punctual, self-sufficient and easy to live with, qualities that matter on board a ship or on a long residency.

FAQ

Questions recruiters ask

How long are the sets?

I perform 3+ hours of continuous music, with breaks arranged to suit the venue. Set length and genre are fully flexible.

What equipment do you bring?

I travel with my own instruments and a self-contained sound system, set up by myself. A venue PA can also be used if available.

Which genres can you cover?

Classic Rock, Pop Rock, Country, Folk, Blues, Bossa Nova and MPB, with a setlist adapted to the audience.

Are you available for international contracts?

Yes. Send the dates, itinerary and contract terms and I will confirm availability and any documentation needed.

How do I book you?

Use the Book button, email or WhatsApp with the date, venue and audience. I reply with availability and a tailored proposal.
